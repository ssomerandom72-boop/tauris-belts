Our Story

A Legacy of Craftsmanship

Tauris Belts & Custom Leathers was founded in 1978 by Philip P. Taurisano, a former police officer with a vision: create the finest leather products for those who demand quality.

Over four decades, we've handcrafted thousands of belts, holsters, straps, and custom pieces using premium materials and time-honored techniques. Every item carries our hallmark of excellence.
