The Founders' Story

Rooted in Law Enforcement Experience

"Tauris Belts and Custom Leathers was established in 1978 by Philip P. Taurisano, a former Utica, New York police officer. Philip worked alongside his brother, Michael P. Taurisano, who has operated the Tauris Holsters brand since the mid-1960s. While collaborating with Michael, who also served as the Deputy Chief of the Utica Police Department, Philip developed a firm conviction based on his own experience on the force: there was a vital need for every officer and legal weapon carrier to have a high-quality leather belt to properly contain any holster or apparatus carrying their weapon."

Custom Standards

Engineered for Holster Support

Commercial belts fail under weapon weight because they use filler and bonded leather. Every custom piece built in our workshop uses prime full-grain hides engineered to retain structural memory.

10-12 oz Full-Grain Hide

Single and dual-layer bullhide cuts that prevent vertical collapse and roll when carrying holsters, magazine pouches, or duty tools.

  • Zero Bonded Fillers
  • Heavy Bullhide Density

Hand Burnished & Stitched

Edges are bevelled, sealed, and burnished by hand. Dual-layer models are stitched with heavy bonded nylon to ensure lifetime durability.

  • Hand Bevelled Edges
  • Heavy Bonded Thread

Chicago Screw Fastening

Secured with heavy Chicago screws rather than permanent rivets, allowing easy hardware swapping while offering maximum tensile strength.

  • Solid Brass Hardware
  • Swappable Buckle Rigging

Measurement Standard

How We Size Your Custom Belt

Because each belt is hand-cut individually to your body and equipment, never use your trouser/pant size. Measure an existing belt from the buckle fold line directly to the hole you currently use.

1. Measure existing belt from fold to used hole in inches.
2. Add 1 inch if sizing for an Inside-the-Waistband (IWB) holster.
