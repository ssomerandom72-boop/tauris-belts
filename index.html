The Philosophy

An Estate-Level Tradition of Mastery

Originally established in 1978 in Utica, New York by Philip P. Taurisano—a former police officer turned master artisan—our family workshop operates on a strict principle: true luxury cannot be mass-produced.

Carried forward by Philip Taurisano Jr. and deeply rooted in generations of local craftsmanship, every full-grain hide is individually evaluated for natural character and enduring strength before a single cut is made.
